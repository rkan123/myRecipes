
## Ingredients

### Marinade

- 500 g chicken (boneless or bone-in pieces)
- 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- ½ cup thick yogurt (hung curd)
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp white pepper
- 1 tbsp lemon juice

You can absolutely substitute white pepper with black pepper; toasted black pepper is even better. Black pepper gives the dish a more punchy contrast in its flavor. White pepper gives you a smoother flavor with the sauce. 
When you are marinating with lemon juice- do no let your meat sit for  more than 3-4 hours. The acid in the yoghurt+lemon juice is too much for chicken and you can absolutely over tenderize your meat; this will result in your chicken breaking down while cooking and the pieces of meat will look visually stringy. tldr; marinate with yoghurt overnight or marinate yoghurt+lemon juice for 3-4 hours( but marinate for 30 mins minimum if in a pinch) 
### Gravy

- 2 tbsp butter/oil/both
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 2 green cardamom pods, 3–4 cloves, 1 small cinnamon stick
- 2 medium onions, finely chopped (or boiled and blended to a white paste)
- 2–3 green chillies, slit
- 10–12 cashews, soaked and blended to a smooth paste
- ½ tsp coriander powder
- ½ tsp garam masala
- ½ cup fresh cream 
- ¼ cup milk
- 1 tsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek), crushed
- Salt to taste
- Fresh coriander and ginger juliennes, for garnish

## Method

1. **Marinate:** Mix the chicken with the marinade ingredients.
2. **Temper the whole spices:** Heat butter and oil in a handi or heavy pot. Add cumin, cardamom, cloves and cinnamon and fry until fragrant.
3. **Cook the onions:** Add the onions and green chillies. Sauté on medium until soft and translucent, not browned, so the gravy stays pale.
4. **Sear the chicken:** Add the marinated chicken and cook on high for 4–5 minutes until it firms up.
5. **Add the paste and spices:** Stir in the cashew paste, coriander powder and salt. Cook for 2–3 minutes, stirring so nothing sticks.
6. **Simmer:** Add the milk and a splash of water. Cover and cook on low for 12–15 minutes until the chicken is done.
7. **Finish with cream:** Lower the heat and stir in the cream, garam masala and kasuri methi. Simmer 2–3 minutes; don't boil hard or the cream may split.
8. **Serve:** Garnish with coriander and ginger. Serve hot with naan, roti or jeera rice.
